# Emberline gap register — what is missing to execute the full idea

This is the honest inventory. The software simulation is complete enough to
show the mechanism end to end ([03_RESULTS.md](03_RESULTS.md)); almost
everything that would make Emberline a *product that wakes a real
neighbourhood* does not exist yet. Each row says what exists in this
repository ([AUDIT.md](AUDIT.md) is the reference), what is missing, why it
matters, the next step, and the risk if it stays unresolved.

Status vocabulary:

- **Built-simulated** — implemented and measured in software on synthetic data.
- **Partially built** — a software piece exists but the real-world half does not.
- **NOT BUILT** — nothing in the repository; plan only.

Business, hardware and partnership context in this file was supplied by the
team and is labelled *(team context)*; it is not derived from the code.

## Register

| # | capability | status | what exists | what is missing | why it matters | owner / next step | risk if unresolved |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Physical sensor node | **NOT BUILT** | Virtual node model in `emberline/sensors/` (4 synthetic channels). *(team context)* Breadboard prototype in progress: ESP32-S3-DevKitC-1 + BME680/688 + PMS5003, USB-C, prototype BOM ≈ $106. | A node that powers on and streams the 1 Hz CSV contract (`ms,pm1,pm25,pm10,gas_ohms,temp_c,rh,press_hpa`); firmware; serial capture tool; ingestion into the repo. | Every downstream claim rests on a sensor reading that has never been taken. | Hardware teammate: finish breadboard, publish one 24-h ambient CSV into a new `data/real/` (gitignored) with a checksum. | The whole pitch is a simulation; judges and engineers discount it accordingly. |
| 2 | Real sensor data and a real-data classifier | **NOT BUILT** | Synthetic windows, event-split trainer, GBM + CNN, per-confounder evaluation (`emberline/detect/`). Feature and session contracts defined in [02_DATA_PIPELINE.md](02_DATA_PIPELINE.md) §(d). | Event-labelled sessions (ambient, bbq, wood_stove, vehicle, fog, aerosol, supervised-burn smoke); a real-feature extractor (PM2.5 mean/max/slope, log-ratio of gas resistance to a rolling 10-min baseline, RH/temp means); a GBM retrained with session-level split; a separate real-data report. | The synthetic F1 of 0.95 says nothing about real smoke vs a real wood stove; the confounder signatures were authored by the team. | Software: `emberline/realdata/` ingestion + features; team: record sessions, get supervised-burn access. | Field false-positive rate unknown; sirens torn down by neighbours, or real fires missed. |
| 3 | LoRa mesh on real radios | **NOT BUILT** | Discrete-event radio + MAC model and the escalation protocol in `emberline/mesh/`; 14 tests incl. storm suppression, duty cycle, self-heal. | Any radio hardware; range measurements over real terrain; measured packet loss and collision behaviour; duty-cycle compliance on hardware; storm suppression and head re-election validated with real packets. | The infrastructure-independence claim lives or dies on radios working without towers, in smoke, on ridgelines. The model has no fading, one collision domain, no capture effect. | 3–5 node field test with off-the-shelf LoRa modules; log RSSI vs distance and compare to the `mesh` path-loss constants. | Range/topology assumptions wrong by a large factor; mesh partitions in exactly the terrain it is meant for. |
| 4 | Solar + battery power budget | **NOT BUILT** | Health model in `MeshNode.health` uses `battery_v` as a corroboration weight; a low-battery node is part of the demo story. | Solar panel + LiFePO4 sizing; measured node draw (MCU, PM sensor fan, radio TX, siren); autonomy under days of smoke-dimmed sun; charge controller. | A node that dies during the multi-day smoke event it exists for is worse than none; REPORT's all-degraded mesh can never cascade. | Bench measurement of current draw per mode; soak test on the roof through a week of weather. | Fleet-wide health decay silences the siren when it is needed most. |
| 5 | Enclosure and mounting | **NOT BUILT** | Nothing in code. *(team context)* CAD enclosure being designed by a teammate. | Weatherproof enclosure with airflow for the PM sensor, sun/rain shielding for BME680, mast/mount, siren horn aperture, tamper resistance. | Optical PM sensors misread droplets (the fog confounder is real); enclosure design decides the real false-positive floor. | Enclosure v1 printed, node soaked outdoors 2 weeks, data compared with a bare node. | Sensor drift and fog false alarms dominate field data. |
| 6 | Siren hardware and audibility | **NOT BUILT** | Tier ladder decides *when* to chirp/voice/cascade (`mesh/escalation.py`); no audio path exists. | Speaker/siren selection, dB at distance, voice message playback ("smoke, bearing two-two-five"), power draw, audibility study in a real neighbourhood at night with windows closed. | The product *is* the siren; an inaudible or unintelligible alert is a detection product again. | Prototype siren on the bench; measure dB(A) at 10/50/100 m; test the voice message with non-team listeners. | Warning delivered but not heard. |
| 7 | Sensor drift and maintenance model | **Partially built** | Linear PM drift (0.5 µg/m³/day) in `sensors.sample_series`; `drift_score` in node health; the design decision that degraded nodes cannot cascade. | Measured drift of PMS5003/BME680 over months; a baseline-tracking algorithm on the node; a maintenance/replacement schedule and cost; fleet-health telemetry to a maintainer. | Health gating is the answer to "what if one sensor goes insane" only if health is measured, not assumed. | Long ambient recordings from node #1; implement rolling-baseline gas log-ratio (already in the real-feature contract). | Silent degradation → the siren-deaf fleet REPORT.md warns about. |
| 8 | Fire physics sim-to-real | **Built-simulated** with stated limits | Rothermel-inspired CA (`emberline/firesim/`): surface spread, slope and wind factors, urban gate, water. Limits stated in the docstring. | Spotting/ember transport, crown fire, fire–atmosphere coupling, fuel moisture, non-uniform wind; calibration against an operational solver or a real perimeter. | The surrogate can only be as right as its teacher; WUI disasters are frequently spotting-driven. | Compare CA spread on a real landscape (via adapters) with FARSITE/FlamMap or a documented perimeter sequence; record the discrepancy. | Cones that are confidently wrong in the wind regimes that kill people. |
| 9 | Surrogate validation beyond its teacher | **Built-simulated** | UNet surrogate with IoU@+30 0.627 (val) / 0.657 (held-out wind), ECE 0.060, speedup 0.5× vs the toy CA; wind-augmentation study. | Validation against any perimeter the CA did not generate; a speedup benchmark against an operational-grade solver; longer training (the 0.80 target was missed). | The pitch says "physics-trained neural forecaster"; today it reproduces a toy CA at 63 % overlap and is slower than that CA. | After #8: retrain on solver-generated pairs; re-run `surrogate.eval`; report honestly whichever way it lands. | The forecasting story is the weakest measured link; over-claiming it is the fastest way to lose an expert judge. |
| 10 | Real hindcasts on historical fires | **NOT BUILT** | Hindcast harness and 6 hand-authored scenario YAMLs (`foresight/hindcast.py`, `scenarios/`); adapters exist as stubs raising `NotImplementedError`. | Implemented `adapters/usgs.py`, `landfire.py`, `osm.py`; a real ignition point, wind record and first-report time for at least one documented fire; the same YAML format pointed at them. | "Warning minutes gained" is currently measured against a 911 minute the team typed in. One real hindcast is the difference between an illustration and a claim. | Implement the three adapters (3DEP via TNM API, LANDFIRE FBFM40 via LFPS, OSM via osmnx); pick a well-documented WUI fire with public timelines. | Every hindcast number remains unusable in front of a fire professional. |
| 11 | Foresight twin on real terrain / fuel / roads | **NOT BUILT** | Synthetic twin (`worldgen`), routing on a lattice town, CAP polygon in local metres with no datum. | Real DEM, fuel raster, road graph with true capacities and one-way/width attributes, geodetic CRS in the CAP polygon, building footprints with real access. | Evacuation routing on a synthetic grid town proves the algorithm, not the advice. | Same adapters as #10; add CRS handling to `foresight/cap.py`. | Routes that cannot be driven; CAP polygons no county system can ingest. |
| 12 | County alert integration (CAP / IPAWS) | **Partially built (simulated)** | CAP 1.2-shaped draft with `status: Exercise`, validator, outbox (`foresight/cap.py`); the human-approve step is a design principle in code comments. | Any transmission path; a real sender identity; IPAWS/COG onboarding (an authority the team does not have and may never hold directly); an approval UI or workflow for a duty officer; CAP XML rather than JSON. | The alert authority model says official alerts always pass a human; today the human, the channel and the authority do not exist. | Partner conversation with a county EMA to learn their intake (WEA, IPAWS, local siren control); target "draft to their inbox", not "we transmit". | Legal and reputational exposure if anything is ever construed as an official alert. |
| 13 | Routing liability posture | **Partially built** | Every `RoutePlan` is labelled ADVISORY; a static fallback plan always exists; the demo prints routes as guidance to an incident commander. *(team context)* Resident-facing dynamic routing was judged a liability risk. | Written policy on who sees dynamic routes (incident commander vs residents), pre-planned zone routes as the resident-facing default, review by counsel, disclaimer language for any display. | A resident sent down a road the model cut wrongly is the failure that ends the project. | Draft the posture document; keep resident-facing outputs to pre-planned routes plus "prepare to evacuate". | Uninsurable product; partner refusal. |
| 14 | Regulatory: noise ordinances, FCC Part 15 | **NOT BUILT** | 1 % duty cycle appears as a config constant (`mesh.duty_cycle`) with a comment citing FCC Part 15 / ETSI; nothing else. | Verification that the chosen band and power (915 MHz, 20 dBm modelled) are compliant with Part 15.247 for the actual module; local noise ordinance review for siren tests; permits for outdoor test alarms. | A siren test without notice is a public nuisance complaint; an out-of-spec radio is an FCC problem. | Use a certified module; document band/power; contact the town before any audible test. | Test halted; goodwill lost in the very community that must consent to nodes. |

## Minimum path to a real field pilot

Derived from the register above, in dependency order. Each step names the
artifact that proves it (see [05_ROADMAP.md](05_ROADMAP.md) for timing).

1. **Node #1 streams the CSV contract** (#1). Artifact: a 24-h ambient CSV
   plus a 5-minute clip of the serial stream.
2. **Safety protocol signed and a supervised-burn slot booked** (#18, #15).
   Artifact: the signed one-pager and a date.
3. **Event-labelled sessions recorded, including real smoke** (#2). Artifact:
   session manifest with counts per kind.
4. **Real-feature extractor and GBM retrained; separate real report** (#2).
   Artifact: `REPORT_REAL.md` with precision/recall per confounder and FP per
   node-hour, dated.
5. **3–5 node LoRa field test on real terrain** (#3, #14). Artifact: RSSI vs
   distance log, packet loss table, one measured head re-election, band and
   power documented.
6. **Enclosure v1 + power soak** (#4, #5, #7). Artifact: two weeks of outdoor
   data with battery voltage trace and drift comparison against a bare node.
7. **Siren audibility test with notice to the town** (#6, #14). Artifact:
   dB(A) at three distances and a listener comprehension note.
8. **Pilot host community agreement and posture documents** (#13, #15, #18).
   Artifact: letter of support and the routing/liability posture.
9. **One real hindcast through implemented adapters** (#8–#11). Artifact:
   a scenario YAML on real terrain with a documented fire timeline and the
   harness output, reported separately from the synthetic table.
10. **Insurer/utility discovery and a real-BOM unit-economics sheet** (#16,
    #17). Artifact: call notes and the costed BOM.

Steps 1–4 are the "now" of the roadmap; 5–8 the "next"; 9–10 the "later".
County alert integration (#12) is deliberately *after* a pilot: the design
target is a draft in a duty officer's inbox, never a transmission from
Emberline.
